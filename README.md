# Business-Trackering tools
Income, expense, sales, payment tracker and budget manager

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This project presents different spreadsheet-based tracking solution designed to help small business owners organize sales, income, payment, spending and review expenses over time.

PROBLEM:
1. Manuel Recordkeeping Problem
2. Daily income and expense reconciliation problem
2. Difficulty identifying payments status from customers
3. Poor product performance check and verification
4. Difficulty determining how much has been spent and which categories account for the largest expenses.

WHAT I DID:
1. Created structured automated systems for different business records
2. Designed customizable tracking template with fields for transaction dates, descriptions, categories, amounts, and payment details. 
3. I used spreadsheet formulas and summary tables to organize expense records and prepare monthly and category-based reports.
4. Present summaries and report faster in a clear and understandable format


TRACKERS AND KEY FEATURES:

    PRODUCT AND FINANCE TRACKER
1. Daily income and expense recording
2. Summarizes income and expenses by date, month, and category
3. Product category analysis, by total count and revenue
4. Profit / Loss Statement
5. Dashboard: Spending charts, Profit / Loss Statement
6. Inventory balance

    BUDGET MANAGER
1. Collects (Starting balance, Amount spent, Amount saved)
1. Collects budgets details (description, days limit, budget amount, amount spent, balance)
3. Allows budget tracking and provide reports
4. Daily spending summaries

    SERVICE AND PAYMENET TRACKER
1. Structured service transaction records
2. Payment recording and Debt tracking
3. Transaction summary and reports

    STOCK AND INVENTORY MANAGER
1. Sales and purchase recording
2. Product analysis
3. Inventory and Stock validation
4. Dashboard: Selling charts, Profit / Loss statement

    PRICING CALCULATOR SHEET
1. Item and cost prices input
2. Product costing
3. Profit Margin application
4. Unit selling prices generation

TOOLS USED: 
Microsoft Excel, Google sheet, formulas, tables, charts, and dashboard design.

RESULT:
1. The tracker makes expense records easier to maintain
2. Provides a clearer picture of spending patterns
3. Easier payment and debt tracking
4. Supporting budgeting and routine financial reviews
